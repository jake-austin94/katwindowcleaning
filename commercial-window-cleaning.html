Questions people ask

Can you clean buildings over 3 storeys?

Yes. KAT Window Cleaning handles all manner of commercial work, including buildings of 3 storeys and above. Get in touch with the details and we will advise.

Are you insured?

Yes, KAT Window Cleaning carries full public liability insurance.

Can you look after lots of properties for us?

Yes. Landlords, letting agents and organisations that look after many homes can have every property cleaned on a regular schedule, with electronic invoicing.

Yes. We clean windows on new builds before handover, including large bespoke projects such as our work for Clayland Estates.
