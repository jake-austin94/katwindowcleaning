About Us

Hi there! I'm Andrew, the proud owner of KAT Window Cleaning. Based in Watton, I provide top-quality window cleaning services to Watton, Thetford, and the surrounding villages. With over a decade of experience, your satisfaction is my priority.

At KAT Window Cleaning, I prioritize trustworthiness, safety, and exceptional results. Backed by a quality assurance guarantee, I ensure your windows are sparkling clean. If you're not satisfied, let me know within 24 hours, and I'll re-clean them for free. Serving both residential and commercial properties, I utilize safe techniques and state-of-the-art equipment to deliver outstanding window cleaning services.
