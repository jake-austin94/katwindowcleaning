Commercial

Can you clean buildings over 3 storeys?

Yes. KAT Window Cleaning handles all manner of commercial work, including buildings of 3 storeys and above. Get in touch with the details and we will advise.

Are you insured?

Yes, KAT Window Cleaning carries full public liability insurance.

Can you look after lots of properties for us?

Yes. Landlords, letting agents and organisations that look after many homes can have every property cleaned on a regular schedule, with electronic invoicing.

Do you clean new builds?

Yes. We clean windows on new builds before handover, including large bespoke projects such as our work for Clayland Estates.

How do invoicing and payment work?

We send electronic invoices, and accept online and cash payments.

Homes

How often should I have my windows cleaned?

Most homes have their windows cleaned monthly. One-off cleans are available too, just let us know what suits you.

Do you clean the frames and sills too?

Yes. Frames and sills are cleaned on every visit, not just the glass.

Will I know when you are coming?

Yes. You'll get a reminder the day before each clean with a time slot, and a message when it's done.

Gutters

How much does gutter clearance cost?

Gutters are emptied from £50. The final price depends on the size of the property and the length of guttering, and we always confirm it before we start.

How often should gutters be cleared?

Most homes benefit from a clear at least once a year, ideally after the autumn leaves have fallen. Homes near trees may need it more often.

How do you clean the gutters?

Every gutter is inspected and cleaned out by hand, so nothing is missed.

Can I see the results?

Yes. Photos are available on request, so you can see your gutters are clear.

Solar panels

Why clean solar panels?

Dirt, lichen and bird mess block sunlight. Keeping panels clean helps them work as well as they can.

Do you need to go on the roof?

No. Panels are cleaned safely from the ground using specialist equipment.
